Last Bell

Made by Mrs. Spitzlberger

The school has fallen apart. Climb 5 floors to the roof. The game freezes while you read each question, so take your time. A right answer gives you 10 seconds of go time; a wrong one gives only 2 and shows you why. Run and jump over gaps, sparks, and patrol drones, and grab the gold stars. Every floor has its own twist: bounce mats, conveyor belts, crumbling shelves, moving platforms. Zombie Mrs. S chases you the whole way: keep moving, because if she catches you, you lose 2 seconds! 3 right in a row = turbo shoes. 5 in a row = float boots.
Move with ← → or A D. Jump with Space, ↑ or W (or tap the buttons). Answer with 1–4 or tap. N music · M mute · F full screen · Shift+R new game
