Teacher controls

Nothing here changes the student site until you upload the file it makes.

← Student menu

How this works: change anything below, then press Download settings.js at the bottom and upload that file to GitHub. Students see the change within a minute or two. You can come back and change things any time. Whatever is already live loads here automatically.

1Assign (lock students to a skill)

While anything is checked, students can only pick the checked skills. Old bookmarks follow the lock too. Leave all unchecked to unlock everything.

2Suggest a skill (bonus points)

Suggested skills get a star and move to the front. Students earn bonus points for playing them.

% extra points

3Hide a skill

Hidden skills disappear from the student menu. (If you assign skills in step 1, step 1 wins.)

4Release schedule for games

Keep new games hidden and open one or two a week so students keep coming back. New games get a NEW badge for 7 days.

5Where students send their code

Paste the link to your Google Form (the one that asks for name and code). Students get a Send button that copies their code and opens it. Leave empty for now if you have not made it yet.

    7Leaderboard
